( How digital marketing works )

From “nobody knows us” to “welcome back!”

A two-minute scroll story about how a brand gets found, chosen and loved online — and what Digital Dobby does at every step. Meet the cast:

Scroll to play
01/ 08

Scene 01 · The challenge

A great brand nobody can find.

Riya's shop has a great product — but Arjun, her perfect customer, walks right past. He's busy on his phone, like everyone else. This is where most businesses start.

  • Brand audit

Scene 02 · Strategy

First, find the right people.

Maya, your Digital Dobby strategist, studies the market, competitors and data to answer three questions: who is the ideal customer, where do they spend time, and what will make them care?

  • Market research
  • Customer profile
  • Goals & KPIs

Scene 03 · Awareness

Show up where they scroll.

Reels, posts and targeted Meta and YouTube ads put the brand in front of exactly the right people — not everyone, just the ones most likely to buy.

  • Social media
  • Meta & YouTube ads
  • Content

Scene 04 · Search

Be the answer when they search.

When Arjun searches for what Riya sells, SEO and Google Ads make sure her brand is the first answer he sees.

  • SEO
  • Google Ads
  • Local SEO

Scene 05 · Website

Turn visits into action.

A fast, clear landing page gives him every reason to stay. We test versions side by side — the one that converts better wins.

  • Landing pages
  • UX
  • A/B testing

Scene 06 · Conversion

Click. Cart. Order!

A smooth checkout and smart retargeting turn interest into a sale. This is where marketing becomes revenue.

  • Checkout optimisation
  • Retargeting

Scene 07 · Loyalty

Bring them back again.

A personalised email brings Arjun back for more. Keeping a customer costs far less than finding a new one — so we make every first order the start of a relationship.

  • Email automation
  • Offers
  • Community

Scene 08 · Growth

Measure, learn, repeat.

Every click is tracked. We report what is working, cut what is not and reinvest in the winners — so growth compounds month after month.

  • Analytics
  • Reporting
  • Optimisation
