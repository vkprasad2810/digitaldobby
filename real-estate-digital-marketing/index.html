How it works

  1. Before launchWe study the micro-market, competing projects and buyer searches, then set targets for leads, cost per lead and site visits.
  2. Launch weekLanding page, tracking and campaigns go live together, with WhatsApp and email replies set up so no enquiry waits.
  3. During the launchWe shift budget daily towards the campaigns and areas that bring real site visits, and pause what brings junk leads.
  4. After each monthYou get a plain report: leads, cost per lead, site visits and what we’re changing next.
