Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Digital Dobby ("we", "us") is a digital marketing agency based in Mumbai, India. This policy explains what information we collect through digitaldobby.com, why we collect it and what rights you have. We keep it short and plain.

What we collect

  • What you send through our contact form: your name, email, company, phone or WhatsApp number, the services you're interested in, your budget range, your message and the page you sent it from.
  • Website usage data: we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site (pages viewed, scrolling, clicks, approximate location, device and browser). It uses cookies. We don't use it to identify you personally.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, discuss your project and send you a proposal you asked for.
  • To improve our website and understand which pages and channels work.

We don't sell your personal data, and we don't add you to a mailing list without asking.

Where it's stored and who processes it

  • Form enquiries are saved in a Google Sheet and sent to our Gmail inbox (Google LLC).
  • Website analytics are processed by Google Analytics (Google LLC).
  • The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security.

These providers may store data on servers outside India.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 24 months after our last conversation, or longer if you become a client and we need them for our work together or for legal and accounting reasons. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it or stop using it. You can also withdraw any consent you gave and nominate someone to act for you. To do any of this, email monika@digitaldobby.com. We'll reply within 30 days.

You can block analytics cookies in your browser settings or with Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we handle your data, email us first at monika@digitaldobby.com and we'll try to resolve it. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Changes

We'll update this page if anything changes and show the new date at the top.
