Shopify SEO Checklist for Indian Stores (2026)

By Digital Dobby · 17 November 2026 · 7 min read

Most Shopify stores in India rely on paid ads for almost every sale. That works until ad costs rise. Organic search is the cheapest customer you’re not getting, and on Shopify a handful of fixable issues usually hold stores back.

This is the checklist we work through when we start on a Shopify store. It’s the same approach we used for Design Letters, a Shopify jewellery brand, where organic traffic grew 45% and the conversion rate rose 20%.

1. Connect Google Search Console first

Before changing anything, add your store to Google Search Console and submit your sitemap. Shopify creates it for you at yourstore.com/sitemap.xml. Search Console shows which pages Google has indexed, which searches you appear for and what’s broken. Without it you’re guessing.

2. Check for duplicate product URLs

Shopify can show the same product at two addresses: /products/gold-hoops and /collections/earrings/products/gold-hoops. Shopify adds a canonical tag that tells Google the /products/ version is the main one, but many themes still link to the longer collection URL from collection pages.

What to do: in your theme’s product card, link to product.url rather than the collection-aware URL, so Google crawls one clean address per product.

3. Give every collection page real words

Collection pages are often the pages that should rank for searches like “silver anklets for women” or “plant protein powder”. Most stores leave them as a grid of products with no text, so Google has nothing to understand.

What to do: write 100–200 helpful words for each important collection (who it’s for, materials, how to choose) and use the words buyers actually search. Put a short version above the products and the rest below.

4. Write product titles people search for

“The Aurora” means something to you. “Gold-plated silver hoop earrings for women” is what a shopper types into Google. You can keep the brand name, but lead with what the product is.

  • Edit the SEO title and meta description under “Search engine listing” on each product.
  • Keep titles under about 60 characters and descriptions under about 155, so they aren’t cut off.
  • Write a unique description for every product. Copying the manufacturer’s text gives Google no reason to choose you.

5. Make the store fast on mobile

Most Indian shoppers browse on phones, often on mobile data. A slow store loses them before the page loads, and Google uses page experience as a ranking signal.

  • Run your homepage and a product page through PageSpeed Insights on mobile.
  • Remove apps you don’t use. Each one can add scripts to every page, even after you stop using it, so check the theme code too.
  • Compress images before uploading, and avoid autoplaying videos above the fold.

For Design Letters, cleaning up apps and assets made pages load 25% faster.

6. Describe your images

Add descriptive alt text to product images (“oxidised silver jhumka earrings with pearl drops”), not file names. It helps Google Images, which is a real source of traffic for fashion and jewellery, and it helps visually impaired shoppers.

7. Check your product structured data

Most modern Shopify themes add product structured data (price, availability, reviews) automatically. Test a product page with Google’s Rich Results Test. If reviews or price are missing, your theme or review app may need a setting turned on.

8. Redirect old URLs when you change them

Renamed a product or deleted a collection? Add a redirect under Online Store → Navigation → URL redirects so the old address sends visitors and Google to the new one, instead of a 404 page.

9. Link your pages to each other

Link from blog posts and collection descriptions to your best products and collections. Internal links help Google find pages and understand which ones matter most.

10. Fix “Crawled – currently not indexed” pages

In Search Console, open Pages and look at pages Google found but didn’t index. They’re often thin pages (empty collections, tag pages, near-duplicate products). Improve them, merge them, or stop linking to them.

How long does Shopify SEO take?

Technical and speed fixes can show results within weeks. Rankings for competitive searches usually build over three to six months, and keep compounding after that. It’s slower than ads, but you stop paying per click for every visitor.
