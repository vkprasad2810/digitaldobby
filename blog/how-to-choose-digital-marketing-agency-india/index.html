How to Choose a Digital Marketing Agency in India: 12 Questions to Ask

By Digital Dobby · 17 November 2026 · 6 min read

Hiring the wrong marketing agency costs more than its fee. You lose months of ad spend, momentum and sometimes your ad accounts. The right one becomes an extension of your team.

Whether you talk to us or anyone else, these are the questions to ask, what a good answer sounds like, and when to walk away.

About results

1. Can you show results for a business like mine?

Ask for case studies in your industry or with a similar problem, with real numbers and a named client. “We grew traffic” isn’t a result. “Organic traffic up 45% over eight months for a Shopify jewellery store” is.

2. Which numbers will you report on, and how often?

Good agencies agree on a few business numbers up front (leads, cost per lead, sales, revenue from search) and report them monthly in plain language. Be wary of reports full of impressions and reach with nothing about leads or sales.

3. Do you guarantee results?

The right answer is no. Marketing depends on your product, price, market and sales follow-up. An agency can commit to the work, targets and honest reporting, but anyone who guarantees “500 leads” or “page one in 30 days” is overselling.

About how they work

4. What will you do in the first 30 days?

Expect an audit, tracking fixes, quick wins and a plan, not “we’ll start posting”. If they can’t describe the first month clearly, they probably don’t have a process.

5. Who will actually work on my account?

In some agencies, senior people pitch and juniors deliver. Ask who you’ll talk to every week and how much experience they have.

6. How do ads, SEO and social connect in your plan?

Channels work better together. Your ads, website, search and social should point to the same goal. If each channel is a separate line item with no link between them, results usually suffer.

7. How fast do you reply, and how?

Agree on response times and a channel (WhatsApp group, email, weekly call). It matters most during a launch or a sale.

About money and ownership

8. What exactly is included in the fee?

Get the scope in writing: channels, number of creatives and posts, landing pages, reports and calls. Check what costs extra.

9. Is ad spend separate, and who pays the platforms?

Ad spend should be separate from the fee, and ideally you pay Google and Meta directly. That way you see exactly what was spent, and nobody adds a hidden margin.

10. Who owns the ad accounts, data and creatives?

You should. Ad accounts, Google Analytics, Search Console, your website and the creatives you paid for should be in your name, with the agency added as a user. If you part ways, you keep everything.

11. What’s the minimum term and notice period?

Three months is a fair minimum, because most channels need time to learn and improve. Avoid long lock-ins with no exit if results don’t come.

12. Can I speak to a current client?

A confident agency will connect you with a client or point you to public reviews and recommendations.

Red flags: when to walk away

  • Guaranteed rankings, followers or leads
  • They want to own or keep your ad accounts
  • No case studies with real numbers
  • Reports with lots of charts but no leads, sales or cost per result
  • A long contract with no clear scope
  • “Buy followers” or fake reviews of any kind
