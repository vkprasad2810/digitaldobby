Real Estate Lead Generation: 9 Channels That Work for Indian Developers

By Digital Dobby · 17 November 2026 · 7 min read

Real-estate leads in India come from many places, and each channel brings a different kind of buyer at a different cost. The developers who sell fastest don’t pick one channel. They combine a few and make sure every lead gets a fast reply.

Here are nine channels that work, what each is good for, and how to use it well.

1. Google Search ads

Best for: buyers actively searching right now (“2 BHK in Thane West”, “new launch in Kharghar”).

Search intent is strong, so leads tend to be more serious, though each click can cost more. Use tightly grouped keywords per micro-market and configuration, and send people to a project landing page, not your homepage.

2. Meta lead ads (Facebook and Instagram)

Best for: reaching buyers early, before they start searching, at a lower cost per lead.

Meta’s instant forms are easy to fill, which also means more casual leads. Add one or two qualifying questions (budget, timeline) and follow up within minutes.

3. Video tours on YouTube and Instagram Reels

Best for: building desire and trust, especially for NRI and out-of-town buyers.

Walkthroughs of the sample flat, amenities and neighbourhood do more than brochures. Use them in ads and retarget people who watched most of the video.

4. Property portals

Best for: volume. 99acres, MagicBricks and Housing.com are where many buyers compare options.

The catch: the same buyer often enquires with several developers at once. Portals work best alongside your own campaigns, not as your only source.

5. A fast project landing page and SEO

Best for: turning paid traffic into enquiries, and earning free traffic over time.

One mobile-first page per project with configurations, price cues, location advantages, amenities, RERA details and one clear enquiry form or WhatsApp button. For Rioga Premium, faster landing pages improved the site score by 50% and helped deliver 200+ leads in two months for most residential launches.

6. WhatsApp

Best for: fast replies and conversations Indian buyers actually prefer.

Add a click-to-WhatsApp button to ads and pages, and send an instant reply with the brochure and a link to book a site visit. Use WhatsApp for people who contacted you, not cold messages.

7. Retargeting

Best for: bringing back people who visited the page or watched a video but didn’t enquire.

Show them something new each time: the floor plan, a resident testimonial, a site-visit weekend, payment plan details.

8. Channel partners and brokers

Best for: reach into local buyer networks, especially for larger launches.

Give partners ready-made creatives and a simple way to register leads, so your digital campaigns and partner leads don’t compete with each other.

9. Your own database

Best for: the cheapest leads you’ll get: past enquiries, site visitors and existing customers.

Announce new launches to people who enquired before (with their consent), and run referral offers for existing customers. These leads already know your brand.

Don’t forget RERA

Every ad must carry the project’s RERA registration details. In Maharashtra, MahaRERA has required the registration number and a QR code linking to the project’s MahaRERA page on all project ads since August 2023, across print, digital and social media, and asked for them to be shown prominently in April 2025. It has fined hundreds of projects for missing them. Check your state authority’s current rules before launch.

Which channels should you start with?

For most launches: Google Search plus Meta lead ads, a fast landing page and instant WhatsApp replies. Add portals for volume and retargeting once traffic builds. Measure every channel by site visits and bookings, not just lead count.
