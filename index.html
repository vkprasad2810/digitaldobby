( 05 — FAQ )

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Drop us a line.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Both B2B and B2C — across FMCG and food & beverage, technology and SaaS, real estate, e-commerce, healthcare & wellness, spirituality & tourism, ed-tech and entertainment.

Which channels do you manage?

Meta, Google, LinkedIn and YouTube ads, search engine marketing, SEO, social media, email, content and conversion optimisation — run as one connected plan rather than separate silos.

How do you measure results?

Every engagement starts with clear KPIs — leads, conversions, traffic, engagement and ROI — tracked in Google Analytics and your ad platforms, and reported regularly along with what we're testing next.

How much does it cost?

Plans start from ₹30,000 a month, plus ad spend, which you pay directly to the ad platforms. The final price depends on your goals and channels, so pick a monthly budget range in the form below and we'll send a tailored proposal. Not sure yet? Ask for a free audit first.

How soon will we see results?

Paid campaigns can start bringing in leads within weeks of launch — Rioga Premium generated 200+ leads in the first two months of its project launches. SEO compounds over months: organic traffic for Jollywell and Design Letters grew 45% over 8–11 months.

Which tools and platforms do you use?

Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, Google Analytics, Search Console and Tag Manager, SEMrush, Ahrefs and Moz, HubSpot, Mailchimp and Brevo, Hootsuite, Hotjar, Shopify, WordPress, Figma, Canva and Zapier — whatever fits your stack.
